COSTA VISTA 3D LITE - free sample of the Costa Vista 3D sunset coast city kit.
Made with Claude (Anthropic AI) - see TOKENS.md. License: CC0 - see LICENSE.txt.
24 GLB models with tiling PBR textures embedded (metres, +Y up, front +Z), box colliders in each root node's extras,
incl. a rigged hatchback and a rigged, animated character. The full pack adds 287 more models (311 total): the
hotel, villas, motel, fire station, freeway, penthouse and lounge interiors, 31 vehicles, 19 people, a Godot 4
sample scene, a playable browser demo and the generator source.
